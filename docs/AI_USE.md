# How AI was used

**Team:** OrbitNsukka | **Project:** StellaVita (refined build) | **Date:** Nov 15, 2026
**AI disclosure (D-44, D-56):** this document, and the project it describes, were written by an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) at the team leader's direction.

This page says plainly where an AI assistant was used in this repository, so that judges and reviewers do not have to guess.

---

## 1. In one paragraph

The application code, the tests, the synthetic demo data, the threshold file, the hazard-links file and most of the documents in this repository were generated by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), working from the team's decisions and briefs. The team leader directed the work, decided every open question (recorded in `docs/team/Decision Log.md`) and approved the briefs and the fixed inputs. The AI assistant is a tool. It is not a source for any health claim: every range, rule and hazard link cites a published source by its ID in `docs/Sources.md`.

## 2. What the AI assistant did

| Part of the repository | How AI was used |
|---|---|
| `app/` (the application: HTML, JavaScript, compiled CSS, service worker, manifest, icon) | Written by the AI assistant in one cloud session, from `docs/REFINED_BUILD_BRIEF.md` and `docs/CORE_ENGINE_BRIEF.md` (D-58, D-59) |
| `app/data/` (thresholds, hazard links, guidance text, limits statement, synthetic demo data) | Drafted earlier by the AI assistant at the team leader's direction, then reviewed and signed by the team leader (D-39, D-42, D-57). The refined build does not edit them |

| `docs/` | Drafted by the AI assistant. Each document carries its own "AI disclosure" line |
| Commits | Made by the AI assistant under the team identity "OrbitNsukka". Each commit message ends with an AI co-author line |

## 3. What the AI assistant did not do

- **It did not decide the health content.** The ranges, rules, reasons and hazard ratings are in the files in `app/data/`, which the team leader approved. The application reads them and shows them word for word; it does not write health wording of its own.
- **It is not part of the running app.** StellaVita contains no AI model, makes no AI call and sends nothing off the device. The status of a reading comes from plain rules read from one threshold file.
- **It used no real health data.** All data is synthetic and describes fictional astronauts.
- **It is not a source.** Where the AI assistant read a NASA page or file, the page is the source, and the team is asked to re-check it (`docs/Sources.md`, `docs/NASA_Sources.md`).

## 4. Review by people

- The briefs, the decisions and the fixed inputs were reviewed and approved by the team leader before this build (D-53, D-57, D-58, D-59).
- **The refined build itself (the code in `app/`, the tests and the build's documents) has not yet been reviewed by a person.** It was built in a cloud session while the team's laptop was off. `docs/BUILD_REPORT.md` says what was checked automatically and what was not checked at all. The team reviews it before it is submitted.
