# OrbitNsukka Sources File

**Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions
**Local Event:** Nsukka
**Owner:** Data & Science (A.S. lead, O.B. assistant). Cross-checked by Project & Delivery; Data & Science entries are checked by B.B.

This is a general team document. It records every source we use, its license or terms, what we used it for, and its check status. NASA requires every source to be cited, even free or open-source ones, and the license of anything not from NASA or a space agency partner to be stated.

**Status of this file:** DRAFT prepared by the AI assistant on Nov 14, 2026 from the links inside "Human Spaceflight Hazards Research Notes" (dated Nov 13, 2026). Every row is **Logged** only. No link has yet been re-opened for this file, no page title has been confirmed, and no license has been checked unless the research notes themselves stated it. The AI assistant is not a source: each row is a lead for the team to open and verify.

---

## 1. Workflow: verify, then cross-check

1. **Logged.** Anyone (including the AI assistant) adds a row with a link. Nothing Logged may appear in the tool.
2. **Verified.** Data & Science opens the link, confirms the title, publisher, the exact claim it supports and the license, and saves an offline copy. Only the team sets this.
3. **Cross-checked.** Project & Delivery (B.B. for Data & Science entries) confirms the row and that the claim in the tool matches it. Only the team sets this.
4. **Rejected.** Not reliable or not usable. Keep the row and record the reason.

Every claim shown in the tool carries a source ID (S-xx). Every library or dataset used in the build carries a library ID (L-xx). A claim with no ID is **blocked** until it gets one.

## 2. Column notes for this draft

- **Title:** the description used in the research notes. Confirm the real page title when verifying.

- **Offline copy:** "None" for every row. Power and internet may fail during the hackathon, so each Verified source needs a saved copy or short summary, with its location recorded here.
