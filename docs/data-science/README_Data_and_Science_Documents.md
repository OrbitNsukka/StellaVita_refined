## What changed in v0.4 (Nov 14, evening)

v0.3 was signed and frozen as the handoff file (D-42). Core Engine's answers added two rules, so the version was bumped and Integration reruns its checks:

- **Impossible-reading limits (D-48):** heart rate 20 to 250 bpm; systolic 50 to 260 and diastolic 30 to 160 mmHg; blood sugar 20 to 600 mg/dL; temperature 30.0 to 43.0 °C (all team choices); sleep 0 to 24 h; tap 1 to 5; PHQ-2 0 to 6. Outside them the app asks again; the same value twice is accepted and judged.
- **Blood pressure (D-49):** both readings stored, the latest judged.
- **Symptoms (D-51):** entered manually with each reading.
- **Testing (D-50):** one astronaut for workability (SYN-B suggested), all four for consistency.
- **AI disclosure (D-44):** every document carries an AI line; the JSON has `meta.ai_generation`; the CSV has a sidecar `synthetic_demo_data.meta.json`; the script says so in its header.

Check result: no impossible readings in the demo data; colours unchanged from v0.3.

## Known limits

- Red levels are adaptations of public clinical guidance, not NASA flight rules; the demo presents them as educational logic.
- Team choices with no held source, signed by the team leader under D-39: the heart-rate personal range numbers, "a week" for the blood-pressure anomaly flag, "2 days within a week" for repeated high sugar, the standing-drop rule and the two low-temperature lines.
- The daily mood tap is not a validated instrument; the PHQ-2 is a screen, not a diagnosis.
- S-167 stays Logged until a person checks the 2026 table.
