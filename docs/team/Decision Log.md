# OrbitNsukka Decision Log

**Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions
**Local Event:** Nsukka

This is a general team document. Every decision is posted in the team chat with a date and a one-line reason, then copied here. Decisions are never deleted. If one changes, add a new entry that replaces it and mark the old one as "Superseded by D-xx".

**Decision rules (from the charter):** workstream leads decide within their own area. Cross-workstream decisions go to a quick team vote, and the team leader breaks ties. During the hackathon, discussion is time-boxed to about ten minutes.
