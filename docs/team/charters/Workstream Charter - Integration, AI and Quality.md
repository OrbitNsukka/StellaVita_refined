# Workstream Charter: Integration, AI & Quality

**Team:** OrbitNsukka | **Lead:** O.F. | **Assistant:** A.S.
**Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions

Every member takes part in every workstream. Testing of the project starts only after **November 14, 2026, 9:00 a.m.**

## Purpose
Join the pieces together, test the whole flow, and make sure what we submit is correct, sourced and legal.

## Responsibilities
- Join the work of the other workstreams into one tool and test the whole flow.
- Check that every threshold in the tool matches the indicator sheet.
- Do the deeper cross-checks when Project & Delivery needs a technical or quality review.
- Track bugs and fix priorities.
- Check anything the AI assistant produces before it goes into the project, and follow the AI assistant charter.
- Rehearse the demo.

## Additions agreed for this workstream
- **Test checklist:** normal, borderline, critical and missing inputs for all six indicators.
- **Source check:** confirm every claim shown in the tool has a matching entry in the sources file.
- **License check:** confirm every library or dataset used has a recorded open-source or free license.
- **Fresh-device test:** run the tool from scratch on a device with no internet.

## Special documents
- Test checklist and results
- Bug list
- Integration notes
- Check log: what was checked, by whom, and the result

## Rules to enforce
- Never accept an AI-produced claim without a cited source and a Data & Science check. If the assistant is unsure, that uncertainty is recorded and not hidden.
- Stretch features are tested only after the MVP passes all checks.
- No personal information or real health data anywhere in the project.
- Confirm nothing is export-controlled and nothing promotes commercial products.
- Confirm the demo video and submission contain no faces, voices or likenesses of members (all members are under 18).

## Done when
- All checklist items pass, including the offline test.
- Sources, licenses and thresholds all reconcile with the general documents.
