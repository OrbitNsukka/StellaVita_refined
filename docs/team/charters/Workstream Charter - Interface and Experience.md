# Workstream Charter: Interface & Experience

**Team:** OrbitNsukka | **Lead:** B.B. | **Assistant:** A.E.
**Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions

Every member takes part in every workstream. Design and build work starts only at **November 14, 2026, 9:00 a.m.**

## Purpose
Make the tool clear, usable and easy to understand for people who are not experts, and tell the story of the demo well.

## Responsibilities
- Screens for entering the six indicators and reading their status at a glance.
- Color-coded status for each indicator.
- Plain-language explanations, so no one has to assume astronauts know how to assess their health.
- The demo story: one astronaut, one scenario, one full flow from input to status to advice.
- Decide how the demo video shows the app without showing anyone's face or voice.

## Additions agreed for this workstream
- **Accessibility:** never rely on color alone. Pair each color with a word or icon, and keep text readable on small screens.
- **Short in-app instructions:** each indicator gets a simple how-to for measuring it, using content from Data & Science.
- **One-slide pitch:** the problem, the demo, and the science behind it, including the "guidance only, not diagnosis" statement.

## Special documents
- Screen and flow sketches
- Demo script (no faces, no voices of team members)
- One-slide pitch

## Rules to enforce
- **All team members are under 18:** no names, voices, faces or likenesses of members in the demo video, submission or team photo.
- Stretch items (clickable body figure, dynamic charts, reminders, gamification) wait until the team confirms the MVP works perfectly.
- Any image, icon or font used must be free to use and cited, with its license noted.
- No promotion of commercial products or services.

## Cross-check
Routine cross-checks by Project & Delivery. Health wording is checked against Data & Science before it appears on screen.

## Done when
- A person who has never seen the tool can enter data and understand the result.
- The demo shows one complete, clear flow.
