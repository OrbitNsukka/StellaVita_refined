# Workstream Charter: Project & Delivery

**Team:** OrbitNsukka | **Lead:** O.B. | **Assistant:** B.B.
**Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions

Every member takes part in every workstream. This charter says what this workstream owns, so the team stays on schedule and submits on time.

## Purpose
Keep the team organized, on time, rule-compliant and ready to submit.

## Responsibilities
- Run the timeline, task list and daily check-ins.
- Maintain the decision log: every decision gets a date and a one-line reason.
- Do all routine cross-checks of workstream documents against the general documents (B.B. checks Data & Science documents, since O.B. is on that workstream).
- Be the contact for the Local Lead and for questions in Space Apps Connect.
- Own the submission checklist: project page filled in, sources and licenses listed, demo video with no faces or voices, everything done before **November 15, 2026, 11:59 p.m.**

## Additions agreed for this workstream
- **Early-submission backup:** submit a first version early on day two, then improve it, so an outage near the deadline cannot cost the project.
- **Time budget:** set checkpoints for the two days (for example, MVP working by a set hour, demo recorded by another).
- **Guardian logistics:** confirm each member's parent or guardian registration and attendance, and check with the Nsukka Local Lead for extra under-18 requirements.
- **Team Owner duties:** the Team Owner keeps the team page correct and posts the team contact information.

## Special documents
- Timeline and checkpoint plan
- Submission checklist
- Decision log (general document, maintained here)

## Rules to enforce
- No project work before **November 14, 2026, 9:00 a.m.**
- Every decision follows the charter: leads decide in their area, cross-workstream decisions go to a vote, the team leader breaks ties.
- Discussion is time-boxed to about ten minutes during the hackathon.

## Done when
- The first submission is in early on day two, and the final one is in before the deadline.
- The decision log is current and the checklist is fully ticked.
